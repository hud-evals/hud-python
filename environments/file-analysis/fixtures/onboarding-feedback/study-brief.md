# Folio Dock onboarding usability study

This entire study and all participants, quotations and events are fictional.
Goal: decide the next two onboarding improvements before a larger follow-up.
Eight moderated sessions on the same prototype, 2026-10-19. Six first-time
users and two returning users were recruited for convenience, not randomly.
No production funnel, revenue or population conversion data is available.

Tasks, in sequence: (1) create a workspace, (2) invite a teammate,
(3) find a saved customer view. Later tasks are attempted only after task 1.
A successful invitation means the participant found and used the invite flow;
acceptance by an actual teammate was not required. F-07's session disconnected
during task 2: its invitation outcome is unknown and must be excluded from
that outcome denominator. Do not treat unattempted tasks as failures.

Use observed actions and event evidence alongside what participants say.
Recommend exactly two primary changes, note lesser issues, and propose a small
validation study. Do not extrapolate these counts to population conversion or
claim that any change has already improved performance.
